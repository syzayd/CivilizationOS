## Decisions made, and why

- **"Strictly beats every single-signal baseline at recall@10" is gone.** It was measured at a
  19-candidate pool. At 80 the paired difference vs `graph_ppr` is -0.002 with Holm p<0.001:
  statistically significant, practically negligible, so the paper calls the two indistinguishable
  rather than claiming a win or conceding a loss. What replaced it: additive fusion is the only
  method recovering both gold types, and it dominates on `causal@5` (1.00 vs 0.67) at every pool
  size tested. Changed in the abstract, intro, F6 and conclusion.
- **The "near-zero episodic base cannot be multiplied up" mechanism is refuted and now purged from
  five separate places.** N15 measured the root cause's episodic score at 0.96 against a
  distractor's 2.48, and impossibility at ~1 in 200 pairs. The correct claim is Proposition 1c/2:
  multiplicative fusion's promotion threshold scales with the episodic scores of the pair and
  admits no bound in the causal margin, so no single lambda serves every scenario; the additive
  threshold is episodic-independent. **Do not let this drift back.** The fifth instance was found
  only by reading the rendered PDF, after four grep passes had missed it.
- **The paper is positioned as a controlled study, not a competing architecture.** Neighbouring
  systems bundle graph, index, scorer and combination rule and report end-to-end numbers, so
  component contributions are never separated. This paper holds everything but the operator fixed.
  Stated in the abstract, an intro paragraph, and a Related Work paragraph that answers "why not
  just plug additive CombSUM into Graphiti?" head-on, including the cost: we do not run Zep or
  HippoRAG, and transfer of the effect size to a specific deployed system is traded away for
  attribution.
