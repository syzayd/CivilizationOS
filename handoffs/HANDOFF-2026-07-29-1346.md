## Known limitations (accepted, not bugs)

- `TIER2_BUDGET_USD` (default $15) resets on every process restart - Render's free tier sleeps
  after ~15 min idle, so this is a per-process-lifetime cap, not a true daily/monthly ceiling.
- `POST /crisis` cooldown is global (`CRISIS_COOLDOWN_S`, 30s default), not per-IP - one
  determined visitor could still lock out others for 30s at a time, just can't burn the budget
  fast doing it.
- Free-tier OpenRouter models are typically rate-limited (~20 req/day) - if that gets hit,
  debate turns silently fall back to caught-exception behavior (logged, turn skipped), not a
  crash, but visibly degrades the demo. `google/gemini-3.5-flash-lite` was the suggested cheap-
  paid fallback if this becomes annoying.
